Our Humanitarian Travel Strategy

Novustell Travel Management's professionals are experienced with humanitarian and NGO travel, able to secure discounts on preferential humanitarian rates, non-profit fares, flexible cancellations, and accommodations with major carriers.

Our travel experts work with your organization based on needs — whether developing an all-encompassing strategy, updating an existing project, or working on a new proposal — your agent will navigate the logistics of humanitarian travel, determining the best budget-saving strategy, while ensuring safe and convenient travel.

Our Humanitarian Travel department delivers discounted rates on airfare, transportation, meals, and hotels, to clients via access to exclusive contracts combined with consolidated buying power.
